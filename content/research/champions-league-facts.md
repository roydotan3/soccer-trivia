## History: first season, rename, anthem, trophy

- The first European Cup was the 1955-56 season, with 16 teams, conceived in Paris in 1955 as the European Champion Clubs' Cup — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1955%E2%80%9356_European_Cup
- The first ever match was Sporting CP 3-3 Partizan Belgrade on 4 September 1955 in Lisbon — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1955%E2%80%9356_European_Cup
- Real Madrid won the first final 4-3 against Stade de Reims on 13 June 1956 at the Parc des Princes, Paris — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1955%E2%80%9356_European_Cup
- Top scorer of the first season was Miloš Milutinović (Partizan) with 8 goals — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1955%E2%80%9356_European_Cup
- The competition took the name UEFA Champions League in 1992/93 (a group stage was added in 1991 and the new name came with 1992/93) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Champions_League
- 1992/93 was the first season under the Champions League name; Marseille beat AC Milan 1-0 in Munich on 26 May 1993 (Basile Boli header), the first French winners — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992%E2%80%9393_UEFA_Champions_League
- The anthem was written by Tony Britten in 1992, adapted from Handel's "Zadok the Priest", performed by the Royal Philharmonic Orchestra with the Academy of St Martin in the Fields; lyrics are in English, German and French — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Champions_League
- The trophy is nicknamed "Ol' Big Ears" and is 73.5 cm tall and weighs 7.5 kg — https://www.planetfootball.com/champions-league/champions-league-trophy-worth-weight-size-history-who-keeps-it
- The current trophy was designed by Bern jeweller Jörg (also spelled Jürg) Stadelmann, commissioned in 1967, silver, about 340 hours to make; the current version has been used since 2006 — https://www.planetfootball.com/champions-league/champions-league-trophy-worth-weight-size-history-who-keeps-it
- The original cup was donated by the French newspaper L'Équipe; Real Madrid received it permanently and it is displayed in their museum (sources differ on the year: 1966/67 vs after their five straight wins) — https://www.planetfootball.com/champions-league/champions-league-trophy-worth-weight-size-history-who-keeps-it
- Source conflict on who keeps the trophy: Planet Football says since a 2008 rule change UEFA keeps the real trophy and winners get an engraved full-size replica (older rule: keep it after 3 wins in a row or 5 in total); a Bayern profile still describes the older rule — avoid a question on this — https://www.planetfootball.com/champions-league/champions-league-trophy-worth-weight-size-history-who-keeps-it

## Famous and record finals

- Biggest final by total goals: Real Madrid 7-3 Eintracht Frankfurt, 1960, Hampden Park, Glasgow (10 goals) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_European_Cup_and_UEFA_Champions_League_finals
- Other 4-goal final margins: Milan 4-0 Steaua București (1989), Milan 4-0 Barcelona (1994), Bayern 4-0 Atlético in the 1974 replay — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_European_Cup_and_UEFA_Champions_League_finals
- 2005 Istanbul: AC Milan led Liverpool 3-0 at half-time (Maldini 1', Crespo 39' and 44'); Liverpool equalised 3-3 within six minutes (Gerrard 54', Šmicer 56', Xabi Alonso 60') — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_UEFA_Champions_League_final
- The 2005 final was on 25 May 2005 at the Atatürk Olympic Stadium, Istanbul (attendance 69,000); Liverpool won 3-2 on penalties — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_UEFA_Champions_League_final
- In 2005 goalkeeper Jerzy Dudek saved Pirlo's penalty and Shevchenko's decisive kick, and made a double save from Shevchenko in extra time; managers were Rafael Benítez and Carlo Ancelotti — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_UEFA_Champions_League_final
- 2012 Munich: Chelsea beat Bayern Munich 4-3 on penalties after 1-1 on 19 May 2012 at the Allianz Arena (attendance 62,500) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_UEFA_Champions_League_final
- 2012 scorers: Thomas Müller 83' for Bayern, Didier Drogba 88' for Chelsea; Drogba scored the winning penalty and Petr Čech saved Arjen Robben's extra-time penalty — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_UEFA_Champions_League_final
- In 2012 Bayern played in their home stadium (first finalist with home advantage since 1984) and Chelsea became the first London club to win the competition, under Roberto Di Matteo — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_UEFA_Champions_League_final
- 2019 Madrid: Liverpool beat Tottenham 2-0 on 1 June 2019 at the Metropolitano (attendance 63,272), goals by Mohamed Salah (penalty, 2nd minute) and substitute Divock Origi (87th) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_UEFA_Champions_League_final
- The 2019 final was the second all-English final (after 2008), Tottenham's first final, Liverpool's 6th title, Jürgen Klopp's first Champions League win, and the first final with VAR — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_UEFA_Champions_League_final
- 1999 Camp Nou: Manchester United beat Bayern Munich 2-1 (attendance 90,245) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_European_Cup_and_UEFA_Champions_League_finals
- Recent winners: 2020 Bayern 1-0 PSG, 2021 Chelsea 1-0 Man City, 2022 Real Madrid 1-0 Liverpool, 2023 Man City 1-0 Inter (Istanbul), 2024 Real Madrid 2-0 Dortmund (Wembley) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_European_Cup_and_UEFA_Champions_League_finals

## Format (2024/25 onward)

- From 2024/25 a single 36-team "league phase" replaced the 32-team group stage (4 more clubs) — https://de.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/028f-1b7f80216368-4c26e95cc15b-1000--uefa-club-competitions-2024-25-onwards-new-league-phase-
- Each club plays 8 league-phase matches against 8 different opponents (4 home, 4 away), instead of playing 3 opponents twice — https://de.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/028f-1b7f80216368-4c26e95cc15b-1000--uefa-club-competitions-2024-25-onwards-new-league-phase-
- Teams are seeded in four pots by club coefficient and meet two clubs from each pot (one home, one away); the title holder is always top seed in Pot 1 — https://de.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/028f-1b7f80216368-4c26e95cc15b-1000--uefa-club-competitions-2024-25-onwards-new-league-phase-
- UEFA's stated reason: more clubs get a chance, and more competitive, balanced matches throughout the league phase — https://de.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/028f-1b7f80216368-4c26e95cc15b-1000--uefa-club-competitions-2024-25-onwards-new-league-phase-
- Teams cannot face clubs from their own country and can meet at most two clubs from any one other country — https://de.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/028f-1b7f80216368-4c26e95cc15b-1000--uefa-club-competitions-2024-25-onwards-new-league-phase-
- Top 8 go straight to the round of 16, teams 9-24 play two-legged knockout play-offs, teams 25-36 are eliminated; knockout rounds then run two-legged to the single-match final — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026%E2%80%9327_UEFA_Champions_League
