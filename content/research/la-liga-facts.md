## All-time records

- La Liga was founded in 1929; the first season started on 10 February 1929 and ended 23 June 1929 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1929_La_Liga
- The first season (1929) had 10 teams: Arenas, Athletic Bilbao, Athletic Madrid, Barcelona, Español, Europa, Racing Santander, Real Madrid, Real Sociedad and Real Unión — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1929_La_Liga
- Barcelona won the very first La Liga title in 1929, edging Real Madrid after Madrid lost at Athletic Bilbao on the last day — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1929_La_Liga
- Paco Bienzobas of Real Sociedad was top scorer in the first season (17 goals by official reports; 14 per a Marca list) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1929_La_Liga
- Most La Liga titles: Real Madrid 36, Barcelona 29, Atlético Madrid 11, Athletic Bilbao 8, Valencia 6, Real Sociedad 2; Real Betis, Sevilla and Deportivo La Coruña one each — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Liga
- Villarreal have never won La Liga (0 titles) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Liga
- Lionel Messi is La Liga's all-time top scorer with 474 goals — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Liga
- Record La Liga appearances: Andoni Zubizarreta and Joaquín, 622 each — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Liga
- Messi scored 50 La Liga goals in 2011/12, the single-season record — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pichichi_Trophy
- Messi won the Pichichi eight times (all with Barcelona), the most ever — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pichichi_Trophy
- Telmo Zarra (Athletic Bilbao) won the Pichichi six times, the most for a Spanish player — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pichichi_Trophy
- The Pichichi Trophy is named after Athletic Bilbao striker Rafael "Pichichi" Moreno — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pichichi_Trophy
- Record points in a season: 100, by Real Madrid in 2011/12 and Barcelona in 2012/13 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Liga
- Record goals in a season: Real Madrid scored 121 goals in 2011/12 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Liga
- Only Real Madrid, Barcelona and Athletic Bilbao have never been relegated from the top flight — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Liga
- La Liga has had 20 clubs since 1997/98; three go down and three come up each year — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_football_league_system
- Messi's Barcelona senior career ran 2004–2021; he has eight Ballon d'Or awards and now plays for Inter Miami — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lionel_Messi

## El Clásico and rivalries

- Real Madrid and Barcelona have met 264 times officially: 106 wins each and 52 draws (as of 10 May 2026) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Cl%C3%A1sico
- First Barcelona–Madrid meeting: 13 May 1902, Copa de la Coronación, Barcelona 3–1 Madrid FC — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Cl%C3%A1sico
- Biggest Clásico win: Real Madrid 11–1 Barcelona in the 1943 Copa del Generalísimo — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Cl%C3%A1sico
- Messi is the top scorer in El Clásico history with 26 goals; Cristiano Ronaldo scored 18 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Cl%C3%A1sico
- Sergio Busquets has the most Clásico appearances: 48 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Cl%C3%A1sico
- The latest Clásico (10 May 2026) was Barcelona 2–0 Real Madrid, in La Liga — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Cl%C3%A1sico

## Clubs: founding, nicknames, stadiums

- Real Madrid were founded on 6 March 1902 as Madrid Football Club — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_Madrid_CF
- Real Madrid nicknames: Los Blancos (the whites), Los Merengues — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_Madrid_CF
- Real Madrid have won the European Cup/Champions League 15 times, a record (the 15th in 2024) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_Madrid_CF
- Real Madrid's home is the Santiago Bernabéu, opened 14 December 1947, capacity 83,186 after renovation — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estadio_Santiago_Bernab%C3%A9u
- The Bernabéu is named after Santiago Bernabéu, a former Real Madrid player and long-time president — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estadio_Santiago_Bernab%C3%A9u
- Barcelona were founded on 29 November 1899 by Swiss-born Joan (Hans) Gamper — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Barcelona
- Barcelona nicknames: Barça, Blaugrana; fans are Culers; motto "Més que un club" (More than a club) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Barcelona
- Barcelona have won the Champions League five times — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Barcelona
- Camp Nou opened on 24 September 1957; it has been called Spotify Camp Nou since July 2022 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camp_Nou
- [volatile] Spotify Camp Nou capacity is currently 62,652 during rebuilding (planned 105,000); Barcelona returned there on 22 November 2025 beating Athletic 4–0 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camp_Nou
- Barcelona's youth academy La Masia lives in a farmhouse built in 1702 next to Camp Nou — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Barcelona
- Atlético Madrid were founded on 26 April 1903 as Athletic Club Sucursal de Madrid (a branch of Athletic Bilbao) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atl%C3%A9tico_Madrid
- Atlético Madrid nicknames: Los Rojiblancos (red-and-whites), Los Colchoneros (mattress makers) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atl%C3%A9tico_Madrid
- Atlético have won 11 La Liga titles and lost three European Cup/Champions League finals (1974, 2014, 2016) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atl%C3%A9tico_Madrid
- Atlético moved to the Metropolitano in 2017 from the Vicente Calderón (last home game there 21 May 2017) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atl%C3%A9tico_Madrid
- The Metropolitano (original La Peineta) opened on 6 September 1994 and reopened rebuilt on 16 September 2017 (Atlético 1–0 Málaga, Griezmann scored the first goal) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitano_Stadium
- The Metropolitano is officially named Riyadh Air Metropolitano since October 2024; capacity 70,813 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitano_Stadium
- Athletic Club (Bilbao) were founded on 18 July 1898 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athletic_Bilbao
- Athletic Club's nickname is Los Leones (the lions), from the San Mamés church; they wear red-and-white stripes — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athletic_Bilbao
- Athletic Club only field players born or trained in the Basque Country area (cantera policy), in place since about 1911 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athletic_Bilbao
- Athletic have won La Liga 8 times and the Copa del Rey 24 times, second only to Barcelona — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athletic_Bilbao
- Athletic's new San Mamés ("La Catedral") opened 16 September 2013 (3–2 v Celta Vigo); the old San Mamés opened in 1913; capacity about 53,000 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Mam%C3%A9s
- Valencia were founded in 1919; nicknames Los Che and Murciélagos (the bats); Mestalla opened in 1923, capacity 49,430; 6 league titles — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valencia_CF
- Valencia lost consecutive Champions League finals in 2000 (to Real Madrid) and 2001 (to Bayern on penalties) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valencia_CF
- Sevilla were founded on 25 January 1890, play in red and white, and have a record seven Europa League/UEFA Cup titles — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sevilla_FC
- Sevilla's Ramón Sánchez-Pizjuán stadium opened in 1958 (capacity about 43,883); Sevilla's only league title came in 1945/46 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sevilla_FC
- Real Betis were founded on 12 September 1907 (as Sevilla Balompié), play in green and white, and won La Liga once, in 1934/35 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_Betis
- Betis are playing at the 70,000-seat La Cartuja while Benito Villamarín is rebuilt, and lost the 2025 Conference League final 4–1 to Chelsea — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_Betis
- Villarreal were founded on 10 March 1923, are nicknamed El Submarino Amarillo (the Yellow Submarine), play in yellow, and won the 2020/21 Europa League by beating Manchester United on penalties — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villarreal_CF
- Real Sociedad were founded on 7 September 1909, play in white and blue (txuri-urdin), won La Liga in 1980/81 and 1981/82; their stadium is the Reale Arena (Anoeta) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_Sociedad
