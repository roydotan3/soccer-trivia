## Clubs: founding years, nicknames, colours

- Arsenal: founded 1886 (as Dial Square); nickname The Gunners; red shirts with white sleeves; 14 English top-flight titles — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arsenal_F.C.
- Aston Villa: founded 21 November 1874; nicknames The Villans and The Lions; claret and sky blue; won the 1981-82 European Cup (1-0 v Bayern Munich) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aston_Villa_F.C.
- Bournemouth: founded 1899 (as Boscombe), AFC Bournemouth since 1971; nickname The Cherries; red and black — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFC_Bournemouth
- Brentford: founded 10 October 1889; nickname The Bees; red and white stripes — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brentford_F.C.
- Brighton & Hove Albion: founded 1901; nicknames The Seagulls and The Albion; blue and white stripes; reached the 1983 FA Cup final — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brighton_%26_Hove_Albion_F.C.
- Chelsea: founded 10 March 1905; nickname The Blues; royal blue; won the Champions League in 2012 and 2021 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chelsea_F.C.
- Chelsea became the first club to win all four main UEFA competitions after winning the Conference League in 2025 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chelsea_F.C.
- Coventry City: founded 13 August 1883 (as Singers F.C.); nickname The Sky Blues; won the FA Cup in 1987 (3-2 v Tottenham) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coventry_City_F.C.
- Crystal Palace: founded 10 September 1905; nicknames The Eagles and The Glaziers; red and blue stripes; won the 2025 FA Cup (1-0 v Man City), their first major trophy — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystal_Palace_F.C.
- Everton: founded 1878 (as St Domingo's); nicknames The Toffees and The Blues; royal blue; Goodison Park was home from 1892 to 2025 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everton_F.C.
- Fulham: founded 1879; nickname The Cottagers; white shirts, black shorts; called London's oldest professional football club — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulham_F.C.
- Hull City: founded 28 June 1904; nickname The Tigers; black and amber — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hull_City_A.F.C.
- Ipswich Town: founded 16 October 1878; nicknames The Tractor Boys and The Blues; won the league in 1961-62, the FA Cup in 1978 and the UEFA Cup in 1981 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ipswich_Town_F.C.
- Leeds United: founded 17 October 1919; nickname The Whites (also The Peacocks); all white; league champions 1968-69, 1973-74, 1991-92 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leeds_United_F.C.
- Liverpool: founded 3 June 1892; nickname The Reds; all red; 20 English league titles, 6 European Cups — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liverpool_F.C.
- Manchester City: founded 1880 (as St. Mark's); nicknames The Citizens and The Sky Blues; sky blue; named Manchester City in 1894 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_City_F.C.
- Manchester City won their first Champions League in 2022-23, completing a treble — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_City_F.C.
- Manchester United: founded 1878 (as Newton Heath LYR), renamed Manchester United in 1902; nickname The Red Devils; red shirts, white shorts, black socks — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_United_F.C.
- Manchester United won the Premier League, FA Cup and Champions League treble in 1998-99, and were the first English club to win the European Cup (1968) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_United_F.C.
- Newcastle United: founded 1881 (as Stanley F.C.), named Newcastle United in 1892; nicknames The Magpies and The Toon Army; black and white stripes; four top-flight titles (last 1926-27) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newcastle_United_F.C.
- Nottingham Forest: founded 1865; nicknames The Garibaldis and The Reds; won the European Cup in 1979 and 1980 under Brian Clough — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nottingham_Forest_F.C.
- Sunderland: founded 1879; nickname The Black Cats; red and white stripes; six top-flight titles (last 1936) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunderland_A.F.C.
- Tottenham Hotspur: founded 5 September 1882 (as Hotspur F.C.); nicknames Spurs and The Lilywhites; white shirts, navy shorts; first 20th-century club to win the League and FA Cup Double (1961) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tottenham_Hotspur_F.C.

## First season and history

- 1992/93 began on Saturday 15 August 1992 with 22 clubs (reduced to 20 from 1995/96) — https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/58731
- Manchester United won the first Premier League title, 10 points ahead of Aston Villa, their first top-flight title in 26 years — https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/58731
- The first Premier League goal was a header by Brian Deane after five minutes, in Sheffield United's 2-1 win over Manchester United — https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/58731
- 1992/93 top scorer: Teddy Sheringham with 22 goals (Nottingham Forest/Tottenham); he scored the first live televised goal (Forest 1-0 Liverpool) — https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/58731
- Eric Cantona scored the first Premier League hat-trick (Leeds 5-0 Tottenham, August 1992) — https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/58731
- Alan Shearer joined Blackburn from Southampton in 1992 for a British record £3.6 million — https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/58731
- Relegated in the first season: Middlesbrough, Nottingham Forest and Crystal Palace; defending champions Leeds finished 17th — https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/58731
- Fifty-one clubs have competed in the Premier League since it began — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League
